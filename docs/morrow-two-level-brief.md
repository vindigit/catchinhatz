# Morrow Two level brief

Status: spatial design reference for the first playable encounter. The supplied plans establish relationships and routes; they are **not dimensioned construction drawings**. Confirm corridor width, door clearances, stair geometry, and room proportions in the playable blockout.

References: [tower site map](../art/reference/morrow-site.jpg) and [Morrow Two floor and stair plan](../art/reference/morrow-two-plan.jpg).

## Setting and fixed landmarks

Morrow Two is the central tower of the three Morrow Extension slabs, between Morrow One to the north and Morrow Three to the south. The repair yard is east of the site; Mercer Street and the Blue Steps are south. The Split Stack stands east/southeast of Morrow Two. Keep these relationships visible from the exterior approach and selected windows, even if the first mission plays mainly indoors.

The tower has a front/main entry on its south side and a rear/service entry on its north side. Stair A is west of the elevator core, Stair B is east, and two elevators occupy the middle. Elevators are unreliable in the fiction, so the stairwells carry the dependable route and a possible alternate or enemy route. The reference also shows a basement and roof access via Stair A.

## Ground floor

Preserve the plan's useful room functions around a legible lobby: management and package rooms near the front, community room and laundry off the sides, maintenance, mail/package, and utility space toward the rear. The main entry should establish sightlines to the elevators and both stairs. The service entry should be reachable without passing through the entire lobby.

## Residential floors

Floors 2–12 use a repeatable double-loaded corridor with apartments, a central elevator core, and the two stairs at opposite sides. The drawing indicates roughly 10–12 units per typical floor, but its unit labels skip 11; treat the count and apartment labels as illustrative until we lay out a measured floor. Apartments should vary in occupancy and condition. The whole tower should feel lived in rather than uniformly abandoned.

## First playable route

Build a complete encounter from the main south entry through the lobby, up Stair A, across one residential corridor section, into one apartment, and out by Stair B toward the rear service side. The two stairs create a readable spatial loop and let us test vertical navigation, sightlines, camera collision, and an alternate path within a small scope. The exact mission objective and number of hostiles remain open for narrative and playtest decisions.

The initial build needs only the lobby, the relevant portion of Level 2, one apartment, both connected stair segments, and the two exterior entries. We can show the full 13-story shell outside without filling every floor. Later floors should reuse structural modules while changing encounters, access, lighting, and apartment detail so climbing the tower does not become repetition.

## Blockout checks

- The player can move from front to rear and up/down the two stairs without collision or camera traps.
- At least one point in the lobby offers a choice between Stair A, Stair B, and the elevator core.
- Hostile lines of sight are blocked by actual walls and apartment doors.
- Doorways and landings allow aiming, reloading, and reversing direction without fighting the camera.
- The exterior view preserves the north/center/south tower order and the eastern service/industrial edge.
- The encounter has a start, objective, failure/retry, and completion before more floors are added.
